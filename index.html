What to do

    How it works

    1. Find the leaf. Background pixels are removed so only the leaf is measured.
    2. Read the colours. Each leaf pixel is sorted as healthy, yellow, brown, dark or rust-coloured.
    3. Measure the damage. The share of damaged area gives a severity grade from healthy to critical.
    4. Match and advise. Lesion colour and spread suggest the likely condition for your crop, with steps to follow.

    Conditions we screen for

    Season guide for Kolhapur and Western Maharashtra

    Kharif (June–October)

    Warm, humid monsoon weather favours fungal leaf spots, blast and blight in rice, soybean and sugarcane. Scout fields twice a week after continuous rain.

    Rabi (October–February)

    Cool nights and dew favour late blight in tomato and rust in several crops. Water in the morning so leaves dry before night.

    Summer (March–May)

    Heat and dry spells bring whitefly and aphids, which spread yellowing viruses. Check the underside of leaves.

    What this tool cannot do

    KrishiVision is a screening aid. It reads colour and spread, not leaf cells, so similar-looking problems (a nutrient gap versus a virus) can be confused. Before spraying anything, confirm with your nearest Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or taluka agriculture office and follow the label dose.
